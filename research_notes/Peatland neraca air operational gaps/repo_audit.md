## 1. Model structure and equations: which water-balance terms exist, which are missing or crude?

### Takeaway
The repo holds two separate model families. (A) The "physical model" (current): a KHG-level (lumped) water-table ODE with passive drains and a 2D Boussinesq groundwater solver, plus a closed-form KHG "deficit" mass balance. (B) The "bucket" experiments (kept as a record, declared failed): a 1-parameter-group daily bucket fitted to stations. Rain and evaporation are the only atmospheric terms; canals are fixed-head passive drains; there is no runoff, interception, canal-storage routing, subsidence or fire/rewetting dynamics in any run model.

### Cited Findings

**A. Physical model (`src/repeat_neraca/`)**
- Tier 1 lumped, `Sy(z) dz/dt = R - C(z)(z - z_c)`, `C = 12 T(z)/L^2`, R = P - ET, `z_c = -canal_drawdown`, `L = 1/(canal length per area)`. Explicit scheme with sub-stepping; ponded water leaves (`z <= 0`); NaN forcing days hold state. — `src/repeat_neraca/lumped.py` (lines ~1-64), `docs/neraca-air.md` table at line 9-12.
- Optional ET stress: ET multiplied by a linear factor 1 above `et_stress_start` and 0 below `et_stress_end` (registry defaults -1.54 m / -2.85 m, "drained peat" PEATCLSM-Trop thresholds; natural-peat thresholds -0.70/-1.82 m are used via `--et-stress natural`). — `lumped.py: et_factor`, `config/parameters.yaml`, `docs/neraca-air.md` point 5.
- Tier 2 2D: `Sy(z) dh/dt = div(T(z) grad h) + R`, implicit Euler + Picard (max 10 iterations, tol 1e-5), arithmetic-mean face transmissivity, fixed heads on the KHG edge ring (surface minus `river_stage_below_edge` = 0.3 m) and canal cells (surface minus `canal_drawdown` = 1.2 m), ponding removed each step, spatially uniform R (one KHG series) although the solver accepts an (ny,nx) recharge field per step. Default grid 500 m (`neraca_grid_res_m`); a 250 m grid (~100,000 cells) is noted as an hour+ per run. — `src/repeat_neraca/spatial.py`, `src/repeat_neraca/testbed/groundwater.py: solve`, `docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" item 4.
- Peat hydraulics: `PeatTable.urzainki2020` (default; boreal Päivänen 1973 retention curves, tabulated `testbed/data/urzainki2020_peat_tables.csv`, Sy ~0.14 at surface rising to ~0.245 at -2 m, T ~0.59 m2/day at surface), or exponential tropical sets `tropical-small` / `tropical-large` (Sy = 0.6 exp(0.5 z); T = t1 (exp(2.5 z) - exp(-2.5 d)), t1 = 50 or 500 m2/day) from Urzainki et al. 2023, which the repo itself describes as site-calibrated to Merang-Kepayang, not measured. Hard-coded in `TROPICAL_SETS`, not in the registry. — `testbed/groundwater.py` lines ~20-35, `docs/tropical-peat-hydraulic-parameters.md`, `testbed/data/NOTICE-urzainki2020.md`.
- KHG deficit (the only output the repo recommends using): `deficit_mm = max(0, -sum(P-E) - Sy * dz_obs * 10)` over 1 June to last forcing day (<=31 Oct) of one year; `Sy` constant 0.15 (band 0.10-0.20); `dz_obs` = station-mean change between first and last 7 days (stations with >=50% coverage); band over {ERA5, WaPOR} x {Sy 0.10, 0.15, 0.20}; `deficit_m3 = deficit_mm/1000 * area`; also `deficit_months`. It is a mass balance that uses observed water-table change, not a model. — `src/repeat_neraca/deficit.py`, `docs/neraca-air.md` lines 53-80.
- Reported KHG deficit results (2026-06-01 to 2026-10-09, 131 days): Sugihan-Lumpur P-E = -307 mm, water table 11 cm deeper, deficit 290 mm (band 227-296), 1,842 million m3 over 6,348 km2, 51 stations; Kampar-Gaung P-E = +9 mm, 11 cm shallower, deficit 7 mm (0-12), 50 million m3 over 7,115 km2, 78 stations. — `docs/neraca-air.md` lines 75-78.
- Parameter registry: 70-odd parameters in `config/parameters.yaml` (pydantic-validated ranges, scope, sampling type, confidence, source; `snapshot()` with SHA-256; `sample()` for Monte Carlo draws; `with_overrides()` range-checked). Many registry entries are for modules NOT in this repo: `block_*`, `k_leak`, `gate_band_upper/lower` (-0.20/-0.40 m), `pump_hours`, `relay_conveyance_loss`, `relay_front_speed`, `decision_interval_days` (7), `alloc_horizon`, `donor_buffer`, `priority_protected/repeat_burn/village`, `cost_horizon_years`, `terrain_sensitive_threshold`, `subsidence_drained_mean` (2.2 cm/yr), `subsidence_plantation` (4.3 cm/yr), `dtm_accuracy_target`. A grep of `src/` and `scripts/` finds none of `subsidence_*`, `pump_hours`, `alloc_horizon`, `gate_band_*` used by any run code (they appear only in `params.py` and the yaml). — `config/parameters.yaml`, `src/repeat_neraca/params.py`; grep result of this audit.

**B. Bucket experiments (`src/repeat_neraca/bucket/`)**
- `N(d) = P - E` (mm); `z(d) = clip(z(d-1) - (N - k*c)/(10*Sy), 0, D)`; `c = exp(-d/ell)` canal proximity with `canal_scale_m` = 300; `k` drainage coefficient mm/day; start `z0` on 1 April; dry months Jun-Oct. Fitted by grid search on pooled RMSE: `sy_grid` 0.05-3.0, `k_grid` 0-4 mm/day, `z0_grid` 0-40 cm. — `bucket/model.py`, `bucket/run.py: NERACA_DEFAULTS`, `bucket/calibrate.py: fit`.
- Indicators per sub-KHG (`neraca_unit.csv`): `u_wb_daily_mean`, `u_deficit_months_dry`, `u_dry_days_mean`, `u_storage_decline_pct`, `u_wt_peak_cm`, `u_wt_days_gt40`. — `bucket/model.py: unit_indicators`, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 29-38. Explicitly "No forest or interception term" in the bucket (line 27).
- Canal-level model: `Sy dz/dt = (E - P) + (zc - z)/gamma`, one fitted `zc` per concession (constant, or one level for Apr-May and one for Jun-Oct), Sy and gamma shared. Concession read from SiTMAT station id. — `bucket/canal_level.py` docstring, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 132-141.
- Team's "Kahayan-Sebangau" balance: `WB = P(1 - interception)(1 - runoff coeff) - ET`, monthly, interception 0-16.4% by WorldCover class, runoff coefficient 0.12 (range 0.07-0.18, Pulau Padang), deficit months counted. Exists only as an evaluation script (`bucket/balance_compare.py`) for April-October 2019-2021; the physical model does not include runoff or interception. — `bucket/config.py` (`runoff_coefficient`, `interception_by_class`), `docs/references.md` section 1.

**Term-by-term status**
- P (rain): modeled as input only, ERA5 0.25 degree KHG-mean series; no gauge/satellite rain bias correction in the delivered runs (CHIRPS test never completed). — see section 2.
- ET: ERA5 evaporation (`E_era5_mm`, ~3.9 mm/day mean) or WaPOR v3 300 m (`E_wapor_mm`; the CSV shows it as a dekadal-constant value, e.g. 3.345 for the first days of Kampar-Gaung); depth-dependent ET stress optional in Tier 1/2; no land-cover-specific ET, no interception, no transpiration/soil split. — `_data/kampar_gaung/forcing_daily.csv` header and first rows, `lumped.py`.
- Runoff / surface flow: not modeled in the physical model (surplus above surface is simply removed: `z = min(z, 0)`), no overland flow or flooding. Runoff coefficient exists only in the bucket-experiment balance comparison.
- Canal/drainage outflow: canals are passive fixed-head cells (2D) or a linear exchange with a fixed canal level (lumped). Canal length by class (primary/secondary/tertiary) is rasterised from vectors. No canal storage, no flow routing, no gates/weirs/blocks, no pumping or tidal inflow in any run model. The synthetic testbed has canal topology (`CanalCells` with upstream links; blocks raise their cell and upstream cells to a crest) used only for the synthetic "truth" and the Urzainki benchmark; the docstring says "The water-sharing model does not use it; it carries its own canal storage model" (that model is not in this repo). — `testbed/canals.py`, `inputs.py: fixed_head`.
- Groundwater / WTD: yes, the central state variable (KHG-mean in Tier 1, gridded in Tier 2, point in bucket). Outputs depth below surface (m negative internally; cm positive down in reports).
- Storage: depth-dependent Sy(z) in the physical model; constant 0.15 in the deficit. In the bucket Sy is a fitted "effective" coefficient that ran to the grid edge (Sy 3.0 vs physical <0.5).
- Subsidence: not modeled; only registry constants (2.2 and 4.3 cm/yr) that no code reads. Peat depth `D` is a static input (the bucket caps z at D per pixel only for the storage-decline indicator).
- Fire / rewetting: no fire model. Hazard thresholds (`hazard_moderate -0.40`, `hazard_high -0.60`, `hazard_extreme -0.85` m; `target_wtd -0.40` m from PP 57/2016) are registry values; code only uses 40 cm for the `u_wt_days_gt40` indicator and for `share_deeper_than_0.4` in the 2D run. The `burn_count`/land-cover fields exist only in the synthetic testbed. Rewetting appears only in the synthetic canal-block benchmark (Urzainki 2020).

### Inferences
- The repo's real, delivered quantitative product is the one-season KHG deficit mass balance (a diagnostic that needs observed water-table change), not a predictive water-balance model. Every predictive component (lumped, 2D, bucket) fails against stations in at least one respect (section 4).
- The registry is a design specification for a wider system (blocks, gates, pumps, relays, allocation, cost) that this repo does not implement; the reader should not assume those modules exist from the yaml.

### Gaps
- Whether `repeat-common`, `re-peat` (hub) or `repeat-prioritisation` / `khg-prioritisation-module` hold the gate/pump/allocation code: not accessible here; only cross-references in docs (`docs/neraca-air.md` line 20, `README.md`).
- Registry rows after `cost_horizon_years` were summarised from a script-parsed listing; not every `source` string was read.

## 2. Inputs and forcing: real vs synthetic vs hardcoded; products, resolution, time step

### Takeaway
Real study-area runs use real products: ERA5 (0.25 deg, hourly, summed to local WIB days), WaPOR v3 (300 m, dekadal) as an alternative ET, SiTMAT in-situ water-table loggers, a DTM and peat-depth raster per KHG, canal vector layers with primary/secondary/tertiary class, Sentinel-1 RTC. Rain/ET is one KHG-mean series (no spatial forcing field in the delivered runs). The synthetic testbed uses a stochastic rain generator and hand-set parameters; the canal level is a single assumed drawdown, not an observed level.

### Cited Findings
- Daily forcing file schema `date,window,P_era5_mm,E_era5_mm,E_wapor_mm`; 840 rows per site = 4 windows: `calibration` 2026-04-01 to 2026-10-15 (198 days; WaPOR present for 193), and baseline years 2019, 2020, 2021 each 2019-04-01..10-31 (214 days, WaPOR all present). KHG-mean on local days (UTC+7). — `_data/kampar_gaung/forcing_daily.csv`, `_data/sugihan_lumpur/forcing_daily.csv`, `bucket/export_inputs.py` docstring. Docs say 2026 data end 2026-10-09/10-15 depending on report (forcing 192 days in `docs/reports/*tier1.md`).
- ERA5 is read through the external package `repeat_common` (`forcing.khg_table`, `sources.SOURCE_DEFAULTS["era5"]`, evap stride 3); "A cache miss reads the cloud (about 11 minutes)" and "each year costs about 11 minutes of reads per KHG". — `src/repeat_neraca/forcing_read.py` docstring, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 109. Cloud read, not a scheduled ingestion job.
- Stations: SiTMAT concession-station loggers, via `repeat_common.tma` (env `SITMAT_PROCESSED`, `tma.path`); flagged readings dropped, >=50% coverage; exported as `stations.csv` (`station_id,lon,lat,concession`; id format `concession/<code>:<station>`) and `stations_daily.csv` (`date,station_id,depth_cm`). Counts on disk: Kampar-Gaung 86 stations, 12,144 readings (2026-04-01..2026-10-15); Sugihan-Lumpur 51 stations, 10,041 readings; Sugihan stations all in 3 concessions (18_SBA, 32_BAP, 33_BMH), Kampar-Gaung in 11 concessions with 34 of 86 in 109_THIP. — `_data/*/stations*.csv`, `docs/bucket_reports/*_canal_level.md`, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 101. The data cover one season (2026) only.
- Static layers (paths only, in config; files not in repo): `khg` polygon (gpkg), `dtm` (e.g. `DTM_Sumatera-002/.../DTM_Sungai_Sugihan_Sungai_Lumpur.tif`), `peat_depth` (`PeatDepth_Sumatera/.../Peat_depth_model_KHG_*.img`, scale 0.01 cm to m), canals gpkg with field `KET_KANAL` and classes Kanal Primer/Sekunder/Tersier, optional `units` (sub-KHG polygons, `subkhg_units.gpkg`). Gridded to a 500 m UTM grid by area-average resampling; DTM gaps filled from the nearest cell, peat-depth gaps filled with the KHG median (Sugihan 1.2 m, Kampar 1.8 m; peat depth valid on only 77% of cells). — `config/neraca/example.yaml`, `src/repeat_neraca/inputs.py`, `docs/reports/neraca_*_tier1.md` line 5.
- Peat-depth raster has a floor of 50 cm for 37% of valid pixels (Kampar-Gaung). — `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 88 and 157.
- Sentinel-1: Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC `sentinel-1-rtc` gamma0 VV and VH, 26 scenes per station, April-October 2026, 3x3 pixels at 60 m around each station (the code constant `RES_M`; the report states 60 m). Used only as a diagnostic, not as a model input. — `bucket/sentinel1.py` lines ~49-56, `docs/bucket_reports/kampar_gaung_sentinel1.md` line 3.
- Covariates for diagnostics: DTM elevation/relief, canal distance and density, river distance, KHG-edge distance, NDMI/NDVI of one year from cache, WorldCover land-cover classes. — `bucket/covariates.py` docstring, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 84.
- Other products: TerraClimate (ppt, aet, monthly ~4 km, ends 2021-12) used only in `balance_compare`; CHIRPS v3 (5 km) reader exists but the comparison was never completed because `data.chc.ucsb.edu` returned HTTP 403 after a 198-file parallel read; GSMaP (0.1 deg) and MOD16 untried (MOD16 needs Earthdata login). — `bucket/balance_compare.py` docstring, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 117-118, 153.
- Canal water level: not observed anywhere. The physical model assumes `canal_drawdown` = 1.2 m below local surface (registry confidence "low", source Urzainki et al. 2020), swept 0.5-1.2 m in reports; the bucket-experiment canal-level model infers one level per concession from the station records (e.g. Kampar-Gaung dry-season levels 28-88 cm; Sugihan-Lumpur 32-36 cm from June after 52-56 cm in Apr-May). `bucket/canal_level.py` is a calibration script, not an input pipeline. — `config/parameters.yaml`, `docs/bucket_reports/*_canal_level.md`.
- Synthetic/hardcoded items: `testbed/forcing.py` two-state Markov rain generator (scenarios normal/moderate/strong El Nino; `et0` 4.17 mm/day plus noise); `testbed/khg.py` synthetic 10 km dome with random canals, 14 blocks, 4 gates, 12 loggers, 3 villages, pumps P1-P3 (capacity 22/100/22 m3/h, hard-coded), land cover; `testbed/fields.py` Gaussian random fields. `tests/test_neraca.py: test_loader_reads_a_made_up_khg` builds a toy KHG for loader tests. — those files.
- External third-party data: Urzainki et al. 2020 peat tables CSV (boreal) with MIT notice; `reference_urzainki2020.py` + `scripts/benchmark_urzainki2020.py` need the Siak data from the blopti GitHub repo (not in this repo). — `src/repeat_neraca/testbed/data/NOTICE-urzainki2020.md`, `scripts/benchmark_urzainki2020.py` docstring.

### Inferences
- "Resolution" of forcing is effectively 25 km (ERA5) for rain; the docs say P-E is nearly the same across a KHG for that reason and that the 25 km grid is "the limit" (`docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 46-49, 130). WaPOR at 300 m is available for ET only.
- Data-ingestion is pull-on-demand through `repeat-common` with local caches and manual shell steps; nothing here schedules or refreshes it.

### Gaps
- Station-level metadata (logger depth reference, measurement frequency, QC flags) is handled in `repeat_common.tma`, not audited.
- ERA5 variable names/definitions (which evaporation variable) are in `repeat_common`, not audited.
- Whether `E_wapor_mm` is dekadal-constant by design: inferred from identical values on consecutive days in the CSV head; not verified in code.

## 3. Spatial, canal routing, water-sharing, scenarios, uncertainty, operational pipeline

### Takeaway
There is a gridded 2D groundwater implementation (500 m, uniform forcing, passive canal cells) but no canal-network routing, no water-sharing/allocation logic, no scenario or operating-rule engine, no uncertainty quantification run, and no operational pipeline (no scheduler, forecasting, API or live dashboard). The registry and testbed anticipate those features but they are not coded here.

### Cited Findings
- Spatial: Tier 2 2D solver on a 500 m metric grid with real DTM, peat depth (spatially varying through `T(zeta, depth)`), canal masks by class and sub-KHG aggregation; run only on Sugihan-Lumpur (not Kampar-Gaung); outputs per-sub-KHG mean water table (28 units), station-cell series, final map array. Result: every free cell follows the same curve (per sub-KHG mean min=median=max = 65 cm). — `src/repeat_neraca/spatial.py`, `docs/reports/neraca_sugihan_lumpur_tier2.md`, `docs/neraca-air.md` point 3 and "Not done yet" item 6.
- Canal network routing: none. Canals are rasterised cells with prescribed head; `testbed/canals.py` implements upstream propagation of block crest levels only for the synthetic world and the Urzainki benchmark. No flow directions, discharge, canal storage, or connection to rivers/tides beyond a fixed edge head (`river_stage_below_edge` 0.3 m). `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` "Not in this version": "Lateral flow and canal outflow from the DTM and flow directions". `docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" item 2: supply term at edge/canals (tide, river inflow) not built.
- Water sharing / allocation / operating rules: not in this repo (the testbed docstring refers to a separate "water-sharing model" with its own canal storage model; the package was previously named `watershare`). Registry params `decision_interval_days`, `alloc_horizon`, `donor_buffer`, `priority_*`, `gate_band_*`, `pump_hours`, `relay_*` are defined but unused by code. The synthetic KHG includes gates, blocks, pumps, water-source intakes and villages as data records only. A test shows the testbed truth solver runs and "blocks help" (`tests/test_khg.py: test_truth_solver_runs_on_the_synthetic_khg_and_blocks_help`).
- Scenario rules: only (i) the synthetic rain scenarios (normal/El Nino) in `testbed/forcing.py`, (ii) CLI sweeps in `scripts/run_neraca.py`: `--k-multiplier 1,10,50,200,1000`, `--drawdown 0.5,1.2`, `--classes`, `--et-stress natural|drained|none`, `--peat-table`, `--tier`, `--res`. No management scenarios (block building, gate operation, pumping) on real KHGs.
- Uncertainty: `Registry.sample()` supports uniform/log-uniform Monte Carlo draws and every param has `range`/`outer_range`, with tests (`tests/test_params.py: test_sampling`), but no script runs a Monte Carlo ensemble on the model. The only uncertainty reported is the deterministic deficit band (ERA5 vs WaPOR x Sy 0.10/0.15/0.20) and sweeps over drawdown, T multiplier, peat table and ET stress. Out-of-fold skill uses 4 spatial blocks (rough, per the docs).
- Operational pipeline: none. No scheduler, no forecast mode, no API, no CI config in the repo (no `.github` or workflow files in `git ls-files`). `bucket/dashboard.py` builds a static HTML explainer page (`docs/bucket_reports/neraca_air_process.html`, `sugihan_lumpur_neraca_air_process.html`) from run outputs and relies on `repeat_common.htmlkit`; it is a report, not a live dashboard. Runs are manual CLI: `python scripts/run_neraca.py --config ... --tier 1|2`, `python -m repeat_neraca.bucket.*`. Outputs are CSV/JSON/MD (`neraca_report.md`, `neraca_khg.csv`, `tier1_series.csv`, `tier2_series.csv`, `neraca_unit.csv`, `registry_snapshot.json`) handed to other modules by file contract (`re-peat/docs/11-data-contracts.md` section C5, per README). — `README.md`, `scripts/run_neraca.py`.
- Time step: daily everywhere (sub-stepped in Tier 1 for stability); window = one dry season of 2026 for stations; no multi-year or climatological simulation with observations. The 2019-2021 forcing windows exist but "the stations only cover 2026" so they are not simulated against data. — `docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" item 6.
- Handoff status: `neraca_unit.csv` columns the ranking reads are only partly written (`u_wt_peak_cm`, `u_wt_days_gt40` exist; deficit indicators "do not yet"), per-sub-KHG outputs get `neraca_skill_ok = 0`. — `docs/neraca-air.md` item 5, `README.md` lines 33-37.

### Inferences
- An operational water-balance/allocation decision-support tool (forecast, scheduled ingestion, operating rules, per-user water shares) would be new work: the pieces present are a calibratable lumped/2D hydrology core, a parameter registry with Monte Carlo support, and a synthetic testbed with structure records.
- Tier-2 gridding adds no spatial information while forcing is uniform and canals are fixed heads at 1.2 m, because the docs show the cells behave as independent buckets.

### Gaps
- The separate "water-sharing model" code (pumps, relays, allocation) is referenced but absent; its status could not be audited.

## 4. Calibration/validation approach, in-situ data, skill metrics

### Takeaway
No parameter is calibrated in the physical model by design ("Nothing is fitted"); it is compared with SiTMAT station water tables. The bucket was fitted by grid search with leave-one-spatial-block-out validation and failed the spatial skill gate. Across all tests, temporal (seasonal timing) skill is moderate-to-good while spatial skill (which stations/places are deeper) is about zero. The best-supported conclusion is that water table is set by management (canal level, weirs), which the forcing cannot supply.

### Cited Findings
**Validation design**
- In-situ data: SiTMAT concession loggers, one season (2026-04-01 to 2026-10-09/15), Kampar-Gaung 86 stations (KHG id 71, 26 sub-KHG), Sugihan-Lumpur 51 stations (KHG 16.02.01, 28 sub-KHG). — `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md`, reports.
- Bucket skill check: per-station temporal correlation; spatial Spearman across stations of simulated vs observed 95th-percentile depth and share of days > 40 cm; leave-one-block-out (k-means on lon/lat, 4 blocks, `kmeans2`); no-skill baseline = mean observed curve of training stations; gate `min_spatial_skill` = 0.3; drainage term kept only if out-of-fold RMSE improves >= 2% and spatial skill does not drop. — `bucket/calibrate.py: cross_validate`, `bucket/run.py: NERACA_DEFAULTS`, `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 51-57.
- Physical model comparison: `validate.py` computes KHG-mean bias, RMSE, RMSE after removing bias and r (first 14 days skipped as spin-up) and station-level pooled bias/RMSE and Spearman of mean level and peak depth; `MIN_SPATIAL_SKILL` = 0.3 sets `neraca_skill_ok` per sub-KHG file only. — `src/repeat_neraca/validate.py`, `scripts/run_neraca.py` line 139.

**Numbers: bucket on Kampar-Gaung (86 stations, 4 blocks)**
- Median per-station temporal correlation 0.63 with drainage term; spatial Spearman held-out -0.03 (peak depth) and -0.02 (days > 40 cm); out-of-fold RMSE 16.5 cm vs 15.3 cm no-skill baseline; verdict "not usable". Fit ran to grid edges (Sy 3.0 vs <~0.5 physical; k 4.0 mm/day; z0 40 cm). Outputs are rescaled covariates: Spearman -0.91 between `u_storage_decline_pct` and mean peat depth across 26 sub-KHG; 0.82 / 0.68 of `u_wt_peak_cm` / `u_wt_days_gt40` with canal proximity. No simple covariate explains deepest stations (Spearman <= 0.34 for peat depth, elevation, canal distance/density, river distance, KHG edge distance, NDMI, NDVI). — `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 72-91.
- Forcing test (`docs/bucket_reports/*_forcing_test.md`): Kampar-Gaung ERA5 rain+ERA5 ET RMSE 20.4 cm without drainage / 16.5 cm with, baseline 15.3, temporal r 0.13 / 0.63, spatial rho (peak, without) -0.10; ERA5 rain + WaPOR ET 24.0 / 18.9 cm, r -0.12 / 0.41, rho -0.09. Sugihan-Lumpur (51 stations): ERA5 11.3 cm (drainage term dropped; table shows 6.4 with), baseline 5.7, temporal r 0.77 without / 0.90 with, rho 0.23; WaPOR ET 14.2 / 7.6 cm, r 0.58 / 0.88, rho 0.21. Both flagged "not usable". Note the README text (lines 113-114) quotes 14.2 vs 11.3 and 7.6 vs 6.4 for Sugihan-Lumpur, consistent with the table.

**Numbers: physical model vs stations (KHG-mean curve; 2026-04-01..10-15)**
- Sugihan-Lumpur, boreal Urzainki table, Tier 1: simulated mean 65.7 cm vs observed 43.0, bias +22.7 cm, RMSE 56.6 cm, RMSE after removing bias 51.8, r 0.96 (drawdown 0.5 or 1.2 m); increasing T x10 to x1000 makes it worse (bias +23.1 to +57.2 cm, RMSE 56.8 to 77.0). — `docs/reports/neraca_sugihan_lumpur_tier1.md`.
- Sugihan-Lumpur with ET stress natural thresholds: bias +16.6, RMSE 46.2, r 0.97; drained thresholds RMSE 56.5. Kampar-Gaung: no change (27.4/27.5). — `docs/reports/neraca_*_tier1_et_*.md`, `docs/neraca-air.md` point 5.

### Inferences
- Skill is real only for the KHG-mean temporal shape (r 0.6-0.97) and for the KHG mass-balance deficit; nothing validates spatial prediction, and absolute level/amplitude depends on assumed canal drawdown and peat table. In Sugihan-Lumpur high correlation (0.96) coexists with bias +23 cm and RMSE 56 cm; the only baseline there (no-skill mean curve) RMSE is 5.7 cm, i.e. the physical model is far worse than simply using the station mean.
- No test uses independent years or an independent KHG; "validation" is in-sample (physical model, unfitted but with parameters chosen in light of the stations) or one-season block CV (bucket).
- Which stations held a managed level is the dominant unmodeled factor; the data reporters themselves conclude this.

### Gaps
- No metrics such as NSE/KGE; skill is RMSE, bias, Pearson r and Spearman only.
- No validation against canal water levels, discharge, gate/pump records, or soil moisture.
- No multi-year water-table observations; no held-out year.
- Several numerical discrepancies between sibling documents were noticed but are small: the README says "the physical result ... 78 stations" for the Kampar-Gaung deficit vs 86 stations elsewhere (different coverage filter); `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 74 says to 2026-10-09 while reports state 10-15.

## 5. The two study areas, and real vs stub/placeholder/synthetic status; tests; TODOs; stated limitations

### Takeaway
Two KHGs are covered: Sungai Kampar - Sungai Gaung (Riau, KHG 71, 7,115 km2, ~26 sub-KHG, 86 stations, mostly acacia/plantation concessions) and Sungai Sugihan - Sungai Lumpur (South Sumatra, KHG 16.02.01, 6,348 km2, 28 sub-KHG, 51 stations, tidal/managed). Run outputs are committed as reports; raw geodata is not. Tests cover numerics of the solvers, parameter registry, deficit arithmetic, loader (toy KHG) and the synthetic testbed, not real-data accuracy.

### Cited Findings
**Study areas**
- Kampar-Gaung: KHG 7,114-7,115 km2; canal length primary 260 km, secondary 1,585 km, tertiary 10,996 km; drain spacing L = 554 m (all three classes); median peat depth 1.8 m; peat depth valid on 77% of cells. — `docs/reports/neraca_kampar_gaung_tier1.md`.
- Sugihan-Lumpur: KHG 6,348 km2; canal primary 782, secondary 3,011, tertiary 10,436 km; L = 446 m; median peat depth 1.2 m; 500 m grid 25,391 cells with 7,588 fixed (30%). — `docs/reports/neraca_sugihan_lumpur_tier1.md`, `..._tier2.md`.
- `config/neraca/example.yaml` is a copy of Sugihan-Lumpur with `khg_id: "16.02.01"`; the real site configs (`re-peat/sites/<site>/neraca.yaml`) are not in this repo. Config paths use `${REPEAT_DATA}` and `${REPEAT_WORK}`.

**Real vs stub vs synthetic**
- Real data runs: Tier 1 both KHG; Tier 2 Sugihan-Lumpur only; bucket, Sentinel-1, canal-level, forcing test, balance compare both KHG; peat-table comparison both KHG. Deficit table both KHG (`neraca_khg.csv` written by `scripts/run_neraca.py`).
- Synthetic: all of `src/repeat_neraca/testbed/{khg,forcing,fields}.py` and tests built on them.
- Placeholder: registry `range` for several hazard thresholds is annotated "range is a placeholder" (`hazard_moderate`, `hazard_high`, `hazard_extreme`); many registry parameters carry `confidence: assumption` (target_share_days, gate/pump/relay/allocation parameters, canal_drawdown `low`, `river_stage_below_edge`, `initial_wtd`, grid res, `hotspot_confidence`). `k_forest`, `k_drained` registry values (30, 5 m/day) are "not wired into the solver" (`docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" item 3).
- Stub-like items: `neraca_unit.csv` writer lacks deficit indicators; `bucket/dashboard.py` and several bucket modules depend on `repeat-common` and on site configs in the external `re-peat` hub, so they will not run standalone; generated reports/HTML "still name the old modules" (README line 12) and `docs/neraca-air.md` says the experiments' site configs "still live in the `repeat-prioritisation` checkout until they move to the hub". `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` references nonexistent paths in this repo (`bucket/forcing.py`, `bucket/README.md`, `../subkhg/README.md`, `docs/data_sources.md`, `docs/neraca_air_references.md`); `bucket/forcing.py` is not in `git ls-files` (forcing code is in `repeat-common`).
- No `TODO`/`FIXME` tokens in `src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/` (grep). Backlog is in prose: `docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" (6 items) and `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` "Future work" (7 items).

**Tests (read, not executed; 11 files, ~60 test functions)**
- `test_neraca.py`: lumped steady state = strip between two drains; lumped matches the 2D solver on the same strip; stability with close drains; drain spacing; registry has neraca params in range; loader reads a made-up KHG; ET factor and ET stress behavior; solver takes recharge field per step.
- `test_groundwater.py`: analytic parabola steady state, mass conservation in a closed domain, symmetry, ponding removal, peat table properties, exponential table reproduces its functions.
- `test_params.py`: registry load/default ranges, depth sign convention, validation, overrides, snapshot hash, sampling.
- `test_deficit.py`: mass balance minus storage change, no deficit when rain covers, rising table adds to deficit, WaPOR bound only if it covers every day, missing days, stop without forcing.
- `test_forcing_read.py`: file route window filter; auto/direct need config block.
- `test_neraca_model.py`: bucket linearity, floor at surface, cap at peat depth, Sy scaling, drainage, many series, NaN hold, indicators.
- `test_canal_level.py`: relaxation to canal level, evaporation deepening, seasonal switch, known levels beat global mean.
- `test_sentinel1.py`: block CV rewards real signal not noise; anomalies per orbit; feature rows.
- `test_khg.py`: synthetic generator determinism, dome, boundary, canals, structures, block upstream rise, land cover, loggers, forcing scenarios, truth solver with blocks.
- `test_urzainki_benchmark.py`: canal rule reproduces stored canal rise; optimised blocks beat rule-based and random (needs external blopti data, likely skipped or data-dependent; not verified).
- Not tested: real-data loading (only a toy KHG), `validate.py`, `run_neraca.py` end to end, `bucket/run.py`, `calibrate.py` cross-validation on real data, forcing readers against live data, `deficit` with real CSVs, Tier 2 `spatial.run`.

**Stated limitations (in docs)**
- "Status: built and tested once ... It failed its own skill check ... its outputs are not used in the ranking" (`docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` line 7). README: bucket "failed its spatial skill check on Kampar-Gaung".
- "A rain-driven balance with canals as passive drains cannot reproduce water tables that are held at 35-55 cm through a dry season. The held level needs water from outside the rain balance and a canal level that is not 'surface minus 1.2 m'." (`docs/neraca-air.md` line 84).
- "Terrain limits any 2D result: the DTM error at 300-600 m is comparable to the head differences." (`docs/neraca-air.md` line 86.)
- Boreal peat table caveat (`NOTICE-urzainki2020.md`, `docs/neraca-air.md` line 66): Sy rises with depth in the table, opposite to tropical measurements; T is 100-1000 x lower than measured tropical conductivities (`docs/tropical-peat-hydraulic-parameters.md`).
- Peat depth raster floor of 50 cm and 77% valid coverage; concession polygons not available (GFW wood-fibre layer "View Only, Not Downloadable"); WorldCover cannot separate acacia/oil palm/natural forest; only 4 spatial blocks; one season of stations; one KHG for most tests. — `docs/bucket_experiments_README.md` lines 102, 155-159.
- Literature review in `docs/references.md` (checked 2026-10-16): three papers read in full (Taufik et al. 2019 Ambio, Mezbahuddin et al. 2023 Sci Rep, Apers et al. 2022 JAMES), others abstract-only; concludes "Every model that works in drained tropical peat treats drainage as an explicit input"; names PEATCLSM-Trop, SWAP and ecosys; notes the team's Kahayan-Sebangau report equations 4.6-4.11, Table 5.10.
- The `re-peat` methodology (design reference in `docs/references.md`): "Tier 1 daily compartment balance coupled to canal storage, depth-dependent specific yield (0.3 to 0.1 at 1.5 m, Ismail et al. 2021), Tier 2 2D Boussinesq model" with canals, structures and logger series as inputs; the repo implements the depth-dependent Sy and the 2D Boussinesq but NOT the canal-storage coupling.

### Inferences
- The repo is best described as a research prototype/diagnostic toolkit in an early (single-commit, no CI, one season of validation) state, with a documented, honest negative result for spatial prediction, rather than a deployable water-balance service.
- The registry is a forward-looking schema for water sharing, gates, pumps, relays and cost optimisation; implementing those would require new code and, per the repo's own findings, observed canal levels and structure operating rules.

### Gaps
- `git log` shows only one commit; history of how results evolved is not available.
- Could not run the tests or any model in this environment (no numpy etc.); numbers are as reported in the committed docs and CSVs, not re-derived. The one exception: forcing and station CSV counts and date ranges were computed with awk from `_data/`.
- The Sugihan-Lumpur 2D tier was not run for Kampar-Gaung, and the `et-stress` option was not run for Tier 2 (`docs/neraca-air.md` "Not done yet" item 1).
